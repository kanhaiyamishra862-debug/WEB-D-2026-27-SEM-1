About College

ABES Engineering College (ABESEC) is a leading engineering institute located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. It offers quality technical and professional education with modern campus facilities and strong placement records.

Courses Offered

1. Undergraduate Programs (B.Tech)

  • Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
  • Computer Science & Engineering (AI & Machine Learning)
  • Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science)
  • Information Technology (IT)
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
  • Mechanical Engineering (ME)

2. Postgraduate Programs

  • Master of Computer Applications (MCA)
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA)
  • M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering

Key Features & Facilities

  • Modern Infrastructure and Well-Equipped Laboratories
  • High Placement Rate in Top IT and Core Companies
  • Dedicated Center for Innovation and Research
  • Spacious Library with Digital Resources
  • Sports Complex and Hostel Facilities
